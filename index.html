K Explore Terms & Conditions

Effective Date: November 2026 | Version 2.4

1. Event Registration & Ticketing

By registering for any event through K Explore, you confirm that the contact information provided (Full Name, Email Address, and 10-digit Indian Mobile Number) is accurate. Registrations are stored locally on your device for immediate verification.

2. Verification & Check-in

Your digital pass reference number (e.g. KEX-2026-XXXX) must be presented upon entry at the event venue. Entry is governed by the respective event organizer's code of conduct and safety regulations.

3. Cancellations & Rescheduling

In the event of cancellation or rescheduling by the organizer, registered attendees will receive immediate email notifications. You can cancel your saved local registration anytime using the "My Tickets" manager.

4. Code of Conduct

K Explore strictly fosters inclusive, safe, and respectful environments for all attendees regardless of background, gender, or orientation.
